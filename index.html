Helpful answers

Questions?
Good.

Don’t see what you need? Talk to us.

What is Rideo?−

Rideo is a Zimbabwean mobility marketplace that connects passengers, drivers and parcels around the routes people already travel.

How does Rideo work?+

Choose a route, see available drivers moving that way, review the details and connect for your journey.

Where is Rideo available?+

Rideo is beginning with a controlled pilot on the Warren Park → Town CBD route in Harare.

How do I become a driver?+

Use the Become a Driver button to apply. We’ll guide you through verification and route setup.

How are drivers verified?+

Verification includes driver licence checks, vehicle inspections and route verification before onboarding.

How do passengers pay?+

Passengers can pay using USD cash, ZWG, or EcoCash digital payments directly through the app.

What is Rideo Parcel?+

Rideo Parcel helps people send packages using verified drivers travelling along the exact route with live step-by-step tracking.

Can drivers deliver parcels?+

Yes. Verified drivers can opt to accept parcel delivery requests when they have available space in their vehicle.
